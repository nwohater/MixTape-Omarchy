# Changelog

## 0.4.0 — 2026-09-19

- Add entire music collections with **+ Folder**, including album subfolders.
- Keep song selections while browsing between folders. **Select all** preserves
  selections from other folders, and **None** clears the full selection.
- Use **Save selected** in the Load browser to prepare a paused queue and open
  the playlist naming form. Save the mix as M3U without starting playback.
- Cover recursive folder additions, cross-folder selection, and saving with
  playback and QML regression checks.
